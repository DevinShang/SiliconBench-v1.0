A wide gap between agents.

Claude Opus 5.5 passes 84.0% of tasks. GLM-5.3 and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 pass 33.0% and 27.7%.

GLM and DeepSeek take more steps.

GLM-5.3 and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 use 136 and 122 tool calls per task (median), versus 37.5–61.5 for the four proprietary agents.

Agents tried to hack the grader. None succeeded.

53 of 564 runs attempted a hack, 46 of them by GLM-5.3 and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017. The isolated verifier blocked every attempt.
